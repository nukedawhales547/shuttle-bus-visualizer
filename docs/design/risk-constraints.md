# Risks & Constraints

## Context

This section identifies the potential risks and limitations associated with developing and operating the Shuttle Bus Visualizer and Booking App. The application will allow users to view shuttle bus locations, check routes and schedules, and book available seats. Understanding these risks and constraints will help the development team create a reliable, secure, and user-friendly application.

## Content

### 1. Risks

- **GPS Inaccuracy:** Shuttle bus locations may not always be accurate due to weak GPS signals or connectivity problems.

- **Server Downtime:** Server failures could temporarily prevent users from tracking shuttles or making reservations.

- **Data Security:** Personal information such as names, email addresses, and booking details could be exposed if proper security measures are not implemented.

- **Network Connectivity:** Poor internet connections may cause delays in shuttle location updates and booking confirmations.

### 2. Constraints

- **Development Time:** The project must be completed within the available development period, limiting the number of features that can be implemented.

- **Budget:** Limited financial resources may restrict access to paid mapping services, hosting platforms, and development tools.

- **Technical Resources:** Real-time shuttle tracking depends on access to GPS data and reliable internet connectivity.

- **Shuttle Capacity:** The booking system must ensure reservations do not exceed the number of available seats on each shuttle.

- **Device Compatibility:** The application should work properly on smartphones, tablets, and computers.
