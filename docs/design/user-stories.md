CSSD 1161 Project - 2026

## User stories

## Overview

User stories provide an example of the demographic our service will benefit aswell as explaining the details as to which upgrades and new features certain people would benefit from on the shuttle bus visualizer app.

## User stories 

**Alex:** A new student at York who lives on Markham campuses residency and uses the shuttle bus app to schedule when to leave his residence to make it to his classes at Markham campus. Due to the shuttle bus apps unclear and sometimes inaccurate shuttle schedule Alex has been late to class twice and missed an entire lecture once this week. When asked how we could improve the Yorku shuttle app, Alex replied "Instead of showing the times the shuttle arrive aproximately throughout the entire day it would be beneficial if we could see a estimated time of arrive for the current shuttles operating incase of a unpredicted time fluctuation".

**Seth:** A student who resides at the Markham campus residence and studies at Markham campus but regularly uses the shuttle bus to attend club meetings and events at Keele campus. He states the shuttle bus is normally delayed but since all he can do is follow the schedule times he sometimes misses the bus and has to wait for the next one or the bus is delayed and he cant tell if he has missed it or if it's runnin late. When asked about what he would benefit from if the app were to be modified he replied " It would be helpful if I could see on the app if the bus is missed or arriving so I know whether I have to wait an hour for the next shuttle or only a couple minutes for it to arrive. This would help very much during the winter seasons, aswell as a destination location since as of right now the shuttle bus shows up at one of two locations, either directly outside the residence building or on the side of the building giving users a 50% chance of missing it that way aswell".

## Requirements

Based on these two user stories there are 2-3 requirements and feature nessecary to add to our new and improved shuttle bus app.

**Requirement 1:** Alex's requested estimated time of arrival for the shuttle buses. This will benefit all shuttle bus users.

**Requirement 2:** Seth's requested shuttle status indicator fo whether or not its en route or missed. This will benefit most frequent commuters.

**Requirement 3:** Seth's requested pick up location pin to identify where exactly he'll be getting picked up from. This will benefit all residence students.
